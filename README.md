## Poorya Shaghaghi

**PhD Researcher** at the [Institute for Energy and Environmental Flows](https://ieef.cam.ac.uk/wps-members/poorya-shaghaghi/), University of Cambridge, and member of [Clare Hall](https://www.clarehall.cam.ac.uk/directory/poorya-shaghaghi/).

### Research areas

Energy Transition · Building Physics · Fluid Mechanics · Heat Transfer · Applied Mathematics

### Current work

I develop reduced-order models of indoor air flows for low-energy building ventilation and passive thermal comfort, including precooling, preheating and thermal mass.

### On this profile

Code and data accompanying my papers, modelling tools for natural ventilation and thermal comfort, and teaching material for undergraduate engineering.
